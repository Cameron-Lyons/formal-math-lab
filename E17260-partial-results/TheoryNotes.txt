Partial structural results for Equation 17260
===========================================

All results in Cancellation17260.lean hold without assuming finiteness.
They do NOT decide existence of nontrivial finite models.

Single-idempotent obstruction (human proof)
-----------------------------------------
Write juxtaposition with explicit parentheses; D(x)=x*x and
T_z(x)=z*(x*D(z)). The law is (y*x)*T_z(x)=x.
Applying the law to T_z(x) with left input x*x gives the general transport
identity T_z(x)=x*T_w(T_z(x)).

Suppose e*e=e. Define P(x)=x*e, Q(x)=e*P(x), H(x)=Q(x)*x.
Specializing the law at z=e gives
  (y*x)*Q(x)=x.                                      (1)
Specializing (1) at x=e gives P(P(x))=e. Specializing at x=P(u) gives
  P(y*P(u))=P(u).
Consequently P(Q(x))=P(x) and Q(Q(x))=Q(x). Transport with z=w=e now gives
  x*Q(x)=Q(x).
Apply (1) to Q(x), with y=x. The preceding two identities imply
  Q(x)*Q(x)=Q(x).                                    (2)

For v=a*x, (1) says v*Q(x)=x. Apply the original law to v with z=Q(x),
using (2), to obtain
  (b*v)*H(x)=v.                                     (3)
For v=T_z(x), use (3) with right input x*D(z), left input a=z and b=D(x).
Since D(x)*T_z(x)=x, this gives
  T_z(x)=x*H(x*D(z)).                                (4)

Put d=D(x), u=D(d). Equation (4) at (d,z=x) says
  x*u=d*H(u)=d*(Q(u)*u)=T_d(Q(u)).
Transport at (u,z=e,w=d) therefore gives Q(u)=u*(x*u).
The original law at (x=d,y=d,z=x) gives d=u*(x*u), so
  d=Q(u).
By (2), every square is idempotent. This is enough to finish, independently
of the preceding construction: if d=x*x and d*d=d, the original law gives
  d=d*(x*d)                 (at (x=d,y=d,z=x)),
  x=(y*x)*(d*(x*d))=(y*x)*d  (at (x,y,z=d)).
Taking y=x gives x=d*d=d. Thus every element is idempotent. This short
step is the Lean lemma subsingleton_of_idempotent_squares.

To finish, an idempotent model satisfies
  (y*x)*x=x,                 (original law with z=x)
  x*(z*(x*z))=x.            (original law with y=x)
Put t=z*(x*z). The first identity gives (x*t)*t=t, while the second gives
x*t=x, forcing t=x. Thus z*(x*z)=x. Applying left multiplication by y to
(x*y)*y=y gives x*y=y. Then z*(x*z)=x forces x=z. The model is trivial.

Square-cycle obstruction
------------------------
If a*a=b and b*b=a, the law at (x=b,y=b,z=a) gives a*b=b.
The law at (x=b,y=a,z=a) then gives b=a. There is an idempotent, so the
model is trivial. Thus every square-map cycle in a nontrivial finite model
would have length at least 3.

Cancellation obstructions
-------------------------
For left cancellation, all T_z(x) agree (cancel the first factor D(x)
in D(x)*T_z(x)=x). Call their common value T(x). Transport and cancellation
give T(T(x))=x*D(x), then T(x)*D(x)=D(x). Also
T(x)=D(x)*T(D(x)). The law at target D(x) with y=T(x) says the latter
expression is D(x), so T(x)=D(x). Hence D(x)*D(x)=D(x) for every x. The short square-idempotent argument
above finishes, so this proof does not need the single-idempotent construction.
For right cancellation, cancel T_z(x) in the law as y varies, then cancel
x in y*x. All elements are equal.

Additional finite counting facts
--------------------------------
Let C_x={y*x:y in A}. For any square q=z*z, if x*q=x'*q and
C_x intersects C_x', then x=x'. Hence column images C_x are disjoint
within each fiber of R_q, implying sum_x |C_x| <= |A|*|C_q|.
This bound is formalized in Finite17260.lean as sum_column_card_le.
Every fiber R_q^{-1}(b) lies inside C_{z*b}.
The directed graph with edges x -> y*x is strongly connected: from x
reach x*x, and from q=z*z reach any a by q -> a*q -> z*(a*q) -> a.
