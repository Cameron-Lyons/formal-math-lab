# Independent review of affine-eliminated branch inputs

The frozen affine-eliminated models pass the independent tuple-polynomial audit in `exact_algorithms_branch_elimination_review.py` and `exact_algorithms_branch_elimination_review.json`, which does not import the exporter. The manifest SHA-256 is `91e25ac8803be65a12998e344060e3e48778e499722f30aaf7b5a663f8b2eaa0`. This proves the reduced models are algebraically equivalent to the four audited branches; it does not compute their unrestricted counts.

For each branch, the checker independently solves its `k + 1` affine equations by Gaussian elimination on the chosen old-coordinate pivots. Each eliminated coordinate becomes an affine polynomial in the surviving coordinates, every original affine equation vanishes after substitution, and projecting onto the surviving coordinates is the identity. Quotienting by the branch's affine equations therefore produces the exact polynomial ring in the surviving variables, with no extra multiplicity.

Before applying any field reduction, the checker expands all 97 old-variable domain identities in each branch as ordinary polynomials over `F3`:

```
(c + sum b_i*y_i)^3 - (c + sum b_i*y_i)
    = sum b_i*(y_i^3-y_i),  over F3.
```

Every eliminated field domain thus already lies in the ideal generated by the surviving domains. Substituting the source moments and elementary cubics, then reducing exponents modulo the kept `y_i^3 - y_i` equations, leaves the ideal unchanged, and the sign-square equation stays untouched. Each reduced quotient is therefore isomorphic to the full branch quotient. Offset and sign stay as independent variables at indices `n - 2` and `n - 1`, so both count multiplier and divisor remain 1.

Across the eight representations, the audit checks 388 ordinary old-variable domain identities, all 1,444 source-generator image certificates, every exported polynomial, all 97 coordinate reconstructions, all 100 canonical affine colors, and all 100 actual-color polynomials. Actual colors keep the bilinear form `a + s*d_cell`, including the `s` term whenever a canonical color has a nonzero constant. It also checks 96 arbitrary free-coordinate round trips and 322 known-board round trips; the symbolic checks for branches 2 and 3 do not need known board witnesses.
