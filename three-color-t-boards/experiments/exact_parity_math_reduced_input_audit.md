# Independent reduced-input audit

**PASS, F₂ only.** Both emitted ordinary-polynomial inputs were checked against
a fresh reconstruction of all 80 Fourier equations from centered T geometry,
without reading the existing reduced JSON.

Gaussian elimination independently recovered the substitutions for cells
`0, 81, 90, 99`, with 80, 17, 80, and 17 summands. Substituting them into all
80 original moments leaves 72 nonzero polynomials; the four unary linear
equations and their four squares cancel to zero. All four eliminated-cell cubic
domains are kept exactly, having `6,321`, `272`, `6,321`, and `272` terms after
reduction modulo the free cubes.

| Emitted mode | Free cubic domains | Pivot cubic domains | Retained moments | Total generators |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Representatives | 96 | 4 | 36 | 136 |
| All moments | 96 | 4 | 72 | 172 |

The all-moment input keeps the full representative input and adds exactly 36
Frobenius partners. Over F₂, squaring swaps exponents 1 and 2 modulo `x_i³+1`,
so those partners already lie in the ideal generated by the representatives and
free cubes. Both inputs therefore define the same quotient.

The four linear eliminations are bijective, and each reconstructed cell still
satisfies its kept cubic domain. The scope remains the **unrestricted labeled
count**, with no prefix, normalization, multiplier, or divisor. Variables are
ordinary polynomial variables, not Boolean bits.

Both `definitions` and `run(..., prepare_only=True)` in the adapter reproduce
the helper files byte for byte, keep separate input and source hashes, and
reject six invalid option combinations. No solver was run.
